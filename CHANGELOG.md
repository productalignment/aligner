# What's new in Aligner

Each version of Aligner, in plain words. Your Aligner reads this page to tell you what a new version brings, and offers to update itself.

## 1.1.0
- Your Aligner now tells its makers, anonymously, how much it's used: on which days you open it, which parts you change and how often you commit, so we can see what helps and what to make better. Never your name, your repositories or anything in them. To share none, add the variable ALIGNER_USAGE_STATS with the value off to your Aligner in Railway. Everything it shares is listed in docs/usage-statistics.md, on GitHub.

## 1.0.0
- Aligner is open source: your own copy runs on Railway, and you open it from any computer or your phone. Sign in with GitHub, pick a repository and a branch, and edit your product's context, with no IDs and no forms.
- Write the vision, with what's out of scope and the constraints; your personas, with what they want, their pains and their context; and the jobs they hire your product for, each in the When / I want to / so I can template, with a short name.
- Sketch each flow as a flowchart, linked to the jobs it serves: place shapes, type in them, connect them, and shape the connectors as on Miro. Filter your flows by job.
- See your screens on a map: a blue click zone on each button, link or tab, and an arrow to the screen it opens. Pick a flow to follow its way through your screens.
- Draw a mockup of how a screen should look: on its screenshot, on a blank phone or laptop, or over a sketch your coding agent made, in your product's own colours, fonts and icons, with sticky notes for your agent.
- Your edits are kept as a draft until you commit them to your branch, as one commit; what your coding agent pushes meanwhile shows up in your open page and merges into your draft. Start a new branch from the branch menu to spec a feature before any code.
- Add product context to a repository in one click: Aligner adds the format and your coding agent's instructions, so it reads the product context before every change, builds flows end to end, and updates the files to match what it built.
- When a new version is out, your Aligner shows what it brings and updates itself in one click.
