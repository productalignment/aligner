# FL-010 Map a product built by prompting
- jobs: JB-006

## Steps
- FS-083: [start-end] Open the repository of a product built by prompting alone, and add its product context
  - screen: SC-007
- FS-084: [user-action] Write the vision, personas and jobs, or ask the agent to draft them from what's built
  - screen: SC-002
- FS-085: [system-action] The agent maps the flows and screens it finds in the code, with screenshots and where each button leads
- FS-086: [system-action] It points out what doesn't fit: flows that serve no job, screens that drifted, clashes with the vision
- FS-087: [user-action] Look over each job with its flows, and the gaps
  - screen: SC-002
- FS-088: [start-end] Keep, fix or drop each gap: the product is back under control
  - screen: SC-002
