# Personas

## PE-001 Busy flatmate
- who: Shares a flat and works long hours
- wants: Do a fair share without having to think about it
- pains: Forgets whose turn it is until someone complains
- context: Checks the phone between shifts, mostly when a reminder arrives

### Jobs
- JB-001: When a chore is due, I want to know it's mine and get it done quickly, so I can do my share without thinking about it
  - name: Do my share
- JB-002: When I'm away, I want to hand my turn to someone, so the flat still runs
  - name: Hand over my turn

## PE-002 Flat organiser
- who: The flatmate who ends up keeping the rota and chasing people
- wants: Stop being the one who chases everyone
- pains: Arguments about who did more, and a rota that falls apart when someone is away
- context: Sets things up once, then checks in every week or two

### Jobs
- JB-003: When someone moves in, I want to set up a fair rota quickly, so nobody has to manage it
  - name: Set up the rota
- JB-004: When someone says they do more, I want to see who did what, so we settle it without an argument
  - name: Settle who did more
