# Product

## Vision
- for: people who share a flat
- who: want chores split fairly without nagging each other
- the: Tidy
- is a: mobile chore planner
- that: rotates chores automatically and reminds the right person at the right time
- unlike: a whiteboard rota or a group chat
- our product: keeps a fair score of who did what, so nobody has to be the flat's manager

## Out of scope
- OS-001: Splitting bills or paying flatmates
- OS-002: Smart-home integrations

## Constraints
- PC-001: No paid third-party services in the MVP
